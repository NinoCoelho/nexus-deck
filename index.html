The problem

Knowledge workers are stuck — paying for AI tools that don't remember, don't act, and don't improve

No memory

Every ChatGPT conversation starts from zero. Re-explain context, re-upload files, re-state preferences. There's no learning loop.

Fragmented tools

ChatGPT + Notion + Zapier + notes + email = $50-100/mo for siloed tools that don't talk to each other.

No autonomous action

Chat AI answers questions but can't schedule tasks, run workflows, or process data while you sleep. No "set and forget."

Privacy & trust gap

Healthcare, finance, government, legal — can't send data to cloud SaaS. Almost no one offers local AI deployment.

The solution

One platform — memory, agents, workflows, content

Nexus replaces your fragmented AI stack with a single workspace that remembers everything, runs autonomously, and self-improves. No code. No drag-and-drop. You describe it, Nexus builds it.
